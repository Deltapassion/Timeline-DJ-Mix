46	The Auto DJ fills the playlist and mixes the tracks on its own. With one folder per style, it plays sets: for example 3 salsas, 3 bachatas, 3 kizombas, then it starts again. Without folders, it picks from the library the track closest in tempo and key.

73	How it works
74	Preview
75	: the ▶ button on each row, in the library as in the playlist, plays the track without stopping the mix. The ⇄ button plays a transition as it will sound. The
76	green line
77	on the timeline shows where the preview is: place it wherever you like by clicking in the bottom strip, Shift+click or right-click. Outputs (headphones on channels 3-4 or on another sound card) are set in "Sound and effects".
78	: volume, EQ and filter per channel, crossfader, echo, flanger and reverb. Editing a waiting track never cuts the one that is playing.
79	: import your files. BPM, beat grid and key are calculated. The green dot marks a key compatible with the track selected in the playlist, a green BPM a tempo within 6 %.
80	: double-click or drag and drop to add, drag rows to change the order. Each track is beat-matched and mixed automatically with the previous one.
81	: drag a block to lengthen or shorten the overlap, pull its edges to cut the start or the end. Click the ruler to place the playhead.
82	Curves
83	: select a block, then click the curve to add a point and drag it. Double-click a point to delete it. Volume, bass and treble each have their own curve.
84	Tempo
85	: during a transition, the tempo glides from one track to the other and both stay in sync. Pitch follows tempo, as on a vinyl turntable.
86	If a track is off-beat
87	: correct its BPM (÷2, ×2 or exact value) and shift its grid in the strip above the timeline.
